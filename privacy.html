Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the Lamp & Lesson website and “Lamp & Lesson Publisher”, a private tool the channel owner uses to publish Lamp & Lesson videos to the channel’s own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Who uses the publisher

The publisher is used only by the owner of Lamp & Lesson, for the owner’s own accounts. It is not offered to other people, and it does not let anyone else sign in.

What data the publisher accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the channel, set their title, description and publishing time, and read view, like and comment counts for the channel’s own videos.
  • TikTok: the account’s display name and ID, permission to upload videos to the account, and the publishing status and basic statistics of those videos.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): access to the Lamp & Lesson Facebook Page and the Instagram account connected to it, to publish videos and read their statistics.

The publisher does not access viewers’ personal data, messages, contacts or any account other than Lamp & Lesson’s own.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner’s own computer and are used only to publish Lamp & Lesson videos and to see how they perform. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

The publisher’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the account settings of each platform, for example Google account permissions. When access is removed, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner’s computer.
