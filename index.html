Lamp & Lesson

A little light from the Bible, every day.

Lamp & Lesson makes short, hand-drawn videos about one beautiful moment in the Bible: a story people rarely hear, a promise for a hard day, or a person who is easy to overlook. Each video takes under a minute, quotes the verse word for word from the Berean Standard Bible, and ends with a quiet thought to carry into the day.

The name comes from Psalm 119:105: “Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”

How the videos are made

Lamp & Lesson is a small independent project. The scripts are written with the help of AI and checked against the Bible text before anything is published, and the narration uses a synthetic voice. Verses are always quoted from the Berean Standard Bible, which is in the public domain. The videos are labelled as AI-assisted on each platform.
